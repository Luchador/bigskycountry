Big Sky Country modifications:
- Excluded the Sun (HYG ID 0) and all records with visual magnitude > 6.5.
- Retained 8,920 records, with HYG IDs recorded alongside each generated entry.
- Converted J2000 right ascension (hours) and declination (degrees) to unit vectors.
- Approximated display colors from observed B-V indices using a blackbody fit
  and CIE color matching. The 40 entries without B-V use neutral white.
- Converted apparent magnitudes to flux, compressed for an SDR display.
- Rounded the generated floating-point values to eight decimal places.

The adapted catalog shared/data/hyg_v41_stars.inc is distributed under
CC BY-SA 4.0. These data terms are separate from the project's source code.
Reproduce the catalog with tools/cook_stars.py and the original CSV.
